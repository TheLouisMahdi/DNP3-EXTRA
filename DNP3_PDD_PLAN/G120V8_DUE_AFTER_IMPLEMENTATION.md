## Not deferred - must be resolved before or during implementation

- RFC5280 X.509 path validation and RFC5755 Attribute Certificate profiles, algorithm/key binding and malformed encodings.
- Applicable IEC/TS 62351-3 revocation rules and approved Host fail-closed policy under SEC-T1-15. The operational service addresses may be provisioned later; the security semantics cannot.
- Supported CA/AA fixture model, approved Method68, IEC 2011 legacy-compatible verifier feature versus production algorithm policy, DNP3-specific RoleID/permission mapping.
- Receiver capacity for a maximum IEC 8192-octet token plus DNP3 APDU and object overhead.
- Exact decision on known A.45.8.3 one-byte vs two-byte textual conflict: follow A.45.8.2 formal two-field body and record contradiction, without claiming an IEEE erratum.
- Do not infer PASS or VERIFIED merely from implementation, document audit, same-project Master/Outstation or a successful codec test.
